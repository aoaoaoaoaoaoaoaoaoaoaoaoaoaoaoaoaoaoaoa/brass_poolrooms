SOOT, STRIKE, AND BRONZE

The judgment target is a legible physical mark, not a comic-book contour. The first study varies only the projected soot band. Later studies replace the composited outline with depressed dies, then hold the winning hybrid die fixed while changing the metal and its room. Click any specimen to move it into the selected and native-gauge benches.

This page is not a fidelity or production judgment surface. Browser font rasterization, SDF reconstruction, analytic crowns, and its WebGL material shader differ from the Rust/egui/WGPU mechanism path. Use the standing Foundry Atelier for actual-size verdicts.

Production currently paints eight fully black one-physical-pixel copies of the glyph around every nonblack face. That is an exact square dilation of a font mask, not soot deposited on three-dimensional relief. It explains both the exceptional acuity and the uniformly inked silhouette.
